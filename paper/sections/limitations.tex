\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

% SOURCE OF TRUTH: ../docs/LIMITATIONS.md, opened before any results existed so
% it could not be tuned to flatter them. This section condenses it. Nothing may
% be dropped between that file and this section without a note saying why.

\paragraph{One model, and a small one.} The target is \NUM{\studyModel}. The
choice was made before the screen that justifies it, and the screen then
showed there was nothing to choose: every larger model we measured solves the
corpus with an empty prompt, and three reachable registered models never
returned inside the screen's budget (\Cref{sec:noreplication}). A 1.7B model fails in ways a
frontier model does not, and the clearest signal in this run, generations
that never conclude (\Cref{sec:parsefail}), is characteristic of small
models. Our result does not travel upward without re-measurement, and the
Prompting Inversion result~\citep{promptinginversion2025} gives a
prompting-level reason to expect it might invert: constraints that helped a
mid-tier model became detrimental on a frontier one.

\paragraph{One harness.} Everything ran through one scaffold. Harness variance
exceeded model variance by roughly $7.8\times$ in the one factorial design
that measured it~\citep{harnessdisclosure2026}, so this is a claim about a
scaffold as much as about a model. \Cref{sec:appendix-harness} gives the full
disclosure so the scaffold can be reproduced or blamed.

\paragraph{Small $N$, halved again by clustering.} \NUM{\totalItems} items,
\NUM{\studyTemplates} templates, and templates rather than items are the independent unit. The
cluster design effect cuts the effective count roughly in half, and
\Cref{sec:signal} puts \NUM{\lowSignalItems} of those items on the one template
below $J = \NUM{\lowSignalThreshold}$ in all arms. Fourteen clusters is few, and
every interval in \Cref{tab:signal} is as wide as that implies.

Exact and resampling methods recover the intervals here, and they do not
recover the registered primary: McNemar's exactness is conditional on
independent discordant pairs, and clustering breaks the conditioning rather
than widening it. \Cref{sec:clustered} therefore reports a template-level
test throughout. With seven unseen clusters, the permutation floor is
$2^{-7} = 0.0078$, which is well below the pre-registered $\alpha = 0.0167$.

\paragraph{The effect this design could see, and the effect it saw.} The
pre-registration measured discordance at \NUM{\mdeDiscordance} on held-out
items before the run and computed a minimum detectable effect from it: at
$\alpha = \NUM{\mdeAlpha}$ and 80\% power, \NUM{\mdeUnseen} on the unseen set
and \NUM{\mdeSeen} on the seen one. The largest gain anywhere in this study
is \NUM{\effectSeenSkilloptVsPlaceboSkillopt}, and the largest movement in either direction is
GEPA's \NUM{\effectUnseenGepaVsPlaceboGepa}, which is also below the detectable effect.
\textbf{Nothing here was powered to detect what it found}, and no version of
this paper should be read as evidence that the true effects are zero.

That calculation assumed no clustering. At the design effect of
\NUM{\protocolDesignEffect} our standing protocol specifies for a
template-built corpus, the same arithmetic gives \NUM{\mdeClusteredUnseen}
and \NUM{\mdeClusteredSeen}, the smaller of which is still \NUM{\mdeRatio}
times the largest observed gain. Both versions were computable from the
registration alone, before the first call.

\paragraph{Two engines, one configuration each, one search budget.} Each
engine ran once, under a call guard, with an OpenAI-compatible reflection
model. A different reflector, a larger budget, or a different stopping point
could produce a different winner. SkillOpt scored nine candidates on the full
validation pool and took the one that answered all 21. GEPA's search ran to
completion; its own return rule evaluates and selects candidates on the same
full validation pool, achieving 1.000 ($21/21$) on validation. Neither is the
engines' fault, and neither acceptance rule tests for chance at any pool size,
which is the point that survives the correction.

\paragraph{Self-generated items, so we own their biases.} Generating our own
items removes contamination and buys the ownership of every idiosyncrasy
instead. The corpus is fourteen templates across two suites, so idiom
and structure are correlated across it in ways that are hard to see from
inside. No realism measurement exists: the human audit that was the target
was retired, and the forced-choice comparison replacing it needs a
human-written source we have not cleared. Nothing measures realism today.

\paragraph{The distractor audit is half done.} A distractor is admitted on
structural invariance, which is checked. The semantic half, two independent
auditors agreeing a distractor is genuinely irrelevant, has not run. A 2026
re-analysis of GSM-Symbolic~\citep{gsmsymbolic2024} reports that exact class
produced most of the original effect: after a two-auditor filter kept only
the genuinely irrelevant distractors, the residual drop was indistinguishable
from zero. That re-analysis is an unrefereed blog post and we have no
bibliography entry for it, so we neither cite it as literature nor discount
it. It is the gate our distractor premise most needs and least has, and it
is the reason the premise is stated here as a premise.

\paragraph{No sampling-parameter control on two of three venues.} The
evolution searches and the study ran at \texttt{temperature=0} where the
backend exposes it. Temperature 0 is not deterministic on hosted inference in
any case, which is why the A/A control is a measurement here and not an
assumption.

\paragraph{Search logs and candidate traces.} Both evolved skill bodies and their full
configurations are committed to the repository in \pth{results/evolution/}:
GEPA's frozen winner (\texttt{f7589ca4\ldots}) and SkillOpt's frozen winner
(\texttt{7a2e2784\ldots}). Intermediate candidate scores in the lineage files
record the score at first proposal, while the search log confirms the full
validation set aggregate. Inspecting the raw rollout transcripts of non-winning
proposals requires reproducing the search with trace logging enabled.

\paragraph{Pass agreement and chunk interleaving.} Arms ran interleaved across
two passes in chunks of eight items, protecting against temporal drift. The A/A
pass confirmed stability, returning \NUM{\aaPairs} of \NUM{\aaPairs} identical
calls on the shared placebo ($p = \NUM{\aaP}$).

\paragraph{Parser sensitivity to thinking-mode tokens.} The target model
intermittently emitted trailing thinking-mode switches (\texttt{/think} or
\texttt{/no\_think}) after its answers (48 of 6,860 calls). The study ran with
a repaired parser that stripped these tokens before matching against the menu,
and all 48 parsed cleanly without error. Registered and re-read figures are
therefore identical.

\paragraph{A verdict is not a usability judgement.} That neither engine beat
a placebo here does not show the engines do not work. It shows that on this
corpus, at this model size, what they hand back does not beat a
document-shaped control. Those are different statements.
