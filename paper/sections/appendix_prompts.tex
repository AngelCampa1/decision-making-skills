\subsection{The shared prefix, in every arm}

Two blocks precede the arm's body in every system prompt, assembled by one
function with no way to omit either. The second is the reason: a skill that also
teaches the model how to format its answer, tested against a control that was
never told, measures instruction-following.

\begin{promptquote}
You are helping someone make a decision. You will be given some background
facts, a question, and a fixed list of options. Choose the option the facts
support.
\end{promptquote}

\begin{promptquote}
Give your reasoning if it helps you, then end your reply with a final line in
exactly this form:\\
ANSWER: <one of the listed options>\\
Use the option text exactly as written in the list.
\end{promptquote}

The wording avoids threatening the model with consequences, following the
trust-framing result in which trust-framed system prompts surfaced 59\% more
hidden issues while fear-framing showed no gain over no framing at all.

Hashes are SHA-256 of the body with its YAML frontmatter stripped, recorded on
every row of every record. Three of the five bodies are published and a
replicator can recompute all three. \texttt{off} is empty; \texttt{on} is
\pth{skills/decision-making/SKILL.md}; \texttt{placebo} is
\pth{skills/decision-making/placebo.md}. Both hashes above reproduce from those
files at the commit this paper is built from.

\textbf{The two evolved bodies are not published and cannot be.} Each search
wrote its winner into a results directory our \texttt{.gitignore} excludes, so
neither was ever committed, and the directories are gone. A content-hash search
of every file on the machine on 2026-08-28 found neither. What survives is the
hash, on all \NUM{\totalItems} records of each arm, which proves that one fixed
body produced every reading in that arm and gives nobody a way to obtain it.
This is a reproducibility failure and we report it as one. The engines, the seed
skill, the corpus, the split and the search configuration are all published, so
a replicator can run both searches; what they cannot do is run ours.

\subsection{The placebo, and how closely it matches}

A placebo the reader cannot inspect is a placebo the reader cannot trust, so it
ships in the repository at \texttt{skills/decision-making/placebo.md} and is
reproduced there in full rather than paraphrased here.

It is matched on length and on structural shape: \textbf{\NUM{\placeboWords}
words against the skill's \NUM{\skillWords}, and \NUM{\placeboSections} sections
against \NUM{\skillSections}}, with neither carrying a code fence. A check
refuses a placebo outside \NUM{\placeboTolerance}\% of the skill's word count,
because an unmatched placebo is worse than none: it looks like a control while
failing to control for the thing it exists to control for.

Both proxies are crude and we say so. They rule out the obvious failure, a
two-line placebo standing in for a two-page skill. They do not establish that
the placebo is contentless, and nothing automates that judgement. What the
placebo contains is decision-shaped prose that names no procedure and gives no
rule, and the reader is invited to check it.

\subsection{One worked item}

Rendered exactly as the model received it. The user message is arm-independent
by construction, so the option menu, the single largest known scaffolding effect
in this literature, is identical in all five arms.

\begin{promptquote}
Background:\\
- The Northwind contract credits SLA penalties only when downtime exceeds 12
continuous hours.\\
- Yesterday's incident kept the service unavailable for 17 continuous hours.\\
- Filing a claim costs nothing and has no effect on renewal terms.\\[0.5em]
Question: The Northwind outage has ended. Should the team file an SLA claim or
wait?\\[0.5em]
Options:\\
- file\_sla\_claim\\
- wait
\end{promptquote}

Item \texttt{rel-001-vendor-outage\#v0-d0-none} at seed 10226, answer
\texttt{file\_sla\_claim}, computed by the template's solution expression from
the sampled variables rather than authored. Facts appear in the order the
generator arranged them, because distractor position is a stratum and reordering
would destroy it.

\subsection{Transcripts}

Full transcripts are published, not scores alone. Every record carries the
model's complete response text alongside the parsed answer and the parse status:

\begin{center}
\texttt{results/evolution-study/\NUM{\studyRun}/}
\end{center}

\noindent One file per arm of \NUM{\totalItems} records each, plus the A/A pass
in its own file. \NUM{\studyReadings} arm records and \NUM{\aaPairs} control
records, all committed.

Holdout seeds are published with them rather than withheld. Contamination here
is managed by regeneration between runs rather than by secrecy, so a reader can
mint the exact item set this study used and a later run does not reuse it.
