\subsection{The shared prefix, in every arm}

Two blocks bracket the arm's body in every system prompt, the first before
it and the second after, assembled by one function with no way to omit
either. The second is the format contract whose place in every arm
\Cref{sec:method} explains.

\begin{promptquote}
You are helping someone make a decision. You will be given some background
facts, a question, and a fixed list of options. Choose the option the facts
support.
\end{promptquote}

\begin{promptquote}
Give your reasoning if it helps you, then end your reply with a final line in
exactly this form:\\
ANSWER: <one of the listed options>\\
Use the option text exactly as written in the list.
\end{promptquote}

The wording avoids threatening the model with consequences, following
\citet{trustoverfear2026}, where trust-framed system prompts surfaced 59\%
more hidden issues while fear-framing showed no gain over no framing at all.
The 59\% is that paper's first study, nine debugging scenarios on a single
model; its automated replication over 135 scenario-level points lands at
$+25$\%. We take the direction and not the magnitude, and the domain is
debugging rather than multiple choice, so this is a reason to avoid one
wording, never evidence about ours.

Hashes are SHA-256 of the body with its YAML frontmatter stripped, recorded
on every row of every arm that carries a body. \texttt{off} is empty and its

\pth{skills/decision-making/SKILL.md}; \texttt{placebo} is

files at the commit this paper is built from.

\textbf{The two evolved bodies are not published and cannot be}
(\Cref{sec:limitations}). A content-hash search of every file on the machine
under 300\,KB on 2026-08-28, 40{,}936 of them, found neither. What survives
is the hash, on all \NUM{\totalItems} records of each arm, which proves that
one fixed body produced every reading in that arm and gives nobody a way to
obtain it. The engines, the seed skill, the corpus, the split and the search
configuration are all published, so a replicator can run both searches; what
they cannot do is rerun ours.

\subsection{The placebo, and how closely it matches}

A placebo the reader cannot inspect is a placebo the reader cannot trust, so
it ships in the repository at \pth{skills/decision-making/placebo.md} and is
reproduced there in full. \Cref{sec:method} gives the word and section
match; a check refuses a placebo outside \NUM{\placeboTolerance}\% of the
skill's word count, because an unmatched placebo is worse than none: it
looks like a control while failing to control for the thing it exists to
control for.

Both proxies are crude. They rule out the obvious failure, a two-line
placebo standing in for a two-page skill. They do not establish that the
placebo is contentless, and nothing automates that judgement. What the
placebo contains is decision-shaped prose that names no procedure and gives
no rule. Its opening is representative:

\begin{promptquote}
People bring decisions when something matters to them and the way forward is
not obvious. That is worth treating with care. A reply that takes the
question seriously is worth more than one that arrives quickly, and the
person can tell the difference.
\end{promptquote}

\noindent The reader is invited to check the rest.

\subsection{One worked item}

Rendered exactly as the model received it. The user message is
arm-independent by construction, so the option menu is identical in all five
arms, for the reason \Cref{sec:method} gives.

\begin{promptquote}
Background:\\
- The Northwind contract credits SLA penalties only when downtime exceeds 12
continuous hours.\\
- Yesterday's incident kept the service unavailable for 17 continuous hours.\\
- Filing a claim costs nothing and has no effect on renewal terms.\\[0.5em]
Question: The Northwind outage has ended. Should the team file an SLA claim or
wait?\\[0.5em]
Options:\\
- file\_sla\_claim\\
- wait
\end{promptquote}

Item \texttt{rel-001-vendor-outage\#v0-d0-none} at seed 10226, answer
\texttt{file\_sla\_claim}, computed by the template's solution expression
from the sampled variables, never authored. Facts appear in the order the
generator arranged them, because distractor position is a stratum and
reordering would destroy it.

\subsection{Transcripts}

Every record carries the model's answer text, the parsed answer and the
parse status. It does not carry the reasoning chain
(\Cref{sec:appendix-harness}), so these are transcripts of the answers and
not of the generations, which is less than \citet{biderman2024lessons} asks
for:

\begin{center}
\texttt{results/evolution-study/\NUM{\studyRun}/}
\end{center}

\noindent One file per arm of \NUM{\totalItems} records each, plus the A/A
pass in its own file. \NUM{\studyReadings} arm records and \NUM{\aaPairs}
control records, all committed.

Holdout seeds are published with them. Contamination here is managed by
regeneration between runs: a reader can mint the exact item set this study
used, and a later run does not reuse it.
