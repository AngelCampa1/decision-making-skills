\section{The Corpus}
\label{sec:corpus}

% SOURCE OF TRUTH: ../docs/PROTOCOL.md sec. 6 and ../docs/EVAL_SET_DATASHEET.md.

\subsection{Generation}

Items are minted from parameterised YAML templates with \emph{computed} ground
truth, in the style of GSM-Symbolic~\citep{gsmsymbolic2024}. Each template
declares variables, a set of short factual statements, a question, a fixed
option menu, and a solution expression evaluated against the sampled variables.
The expression is restricted to a whitelisted abstract syntax tree: no attribute
access, no subscripts, no lambdas.

The argument for this over authored items is auditability. Checking
\NUM{\studyTemplates} template rules is tractable; checking
\NUM{\totalItems} authored answers is not. It also removes the contamination
question, because items are generated fresh rather than drawn from anything a
model could have been trained on. The price is that we own the item biases
instead of inheriting them, which \Cref{sec:signal} is partly about.

Distractors are type-compatible with the decision rule rather than off-topic. An
earlier build used off-topic distractors, the control arm scored at ceiling, and
the diagnosis is in the repository's notebook. A distractor qualifies only if
the computed solution is provably invariant to its removal. Each item is drawn
from a stratum crossing distractor count with distractor position.

\subsection{Splitting, and what it is a split of}

The split is by \emph{template}, not by seed. Template membership is decided by

\begin{center}
\texttt{sha256("evolution-study-v1:"\,+\,template\_id)}
\end{center}

\noindent so it is reproducible and was fixed before either engine ran. Seven
templates were visible to both searches; three were held out entirely.

The alternative, holding out fresh random seeds of the same templates, was the
original plan and was abandoned after a probe showed it would score
memorisation as generalisation: an engine that transcribes a template's decision
rule into the skill body answers every future seed of that template correctly,
and a seed-varied holdout records that as generalisation. Both engines went on
to transcribe template-specific material (\Cref{sec:whatengines}), so the probe
was load-bearing rather than cautious.

A mid-run amendment narrowed one half of this. What the engines lifted were
\emph{constants} and not rules, and a memorised constant does not survive a
seed change here, because every threshold is redrawn per item and restated in
the prompt. So on this corpus the constant channel is closed and holding out
seeds would have caught it. The rule channel is not: each template's comparison
and its load-bearing facts are fixed across every seed, and an engine
transcribing those would still have been scored as generalising. The probe
addressed a failure that remains available and we would run it again.

\subsection{The holdout, minted after the winners were frozen}

Both engines' winners were frozen by content hash first. The test items were
minted afterwards, from seeds at or above 10{,}000, which the harness's
\texttt{assert\_evolvable} refuses to hand to a search. The unseen set is
\NUM{\unseenItems} items over three held-out templates at four holdout seeds;
the seen set is \NUM{\seenItems} items over the seven trained templates at two
holdout seeds. Neither set was ever shown to either engine at any seed.

Corpus identity is recorded as a fingerprint and not a label, because ground
truth here is computed rather than authored and there is no label set to
version. Both fingerprints are in the run manifest.

\subsection{The confound the control arm found in the design}

The two item sets differ in more than being held out. The placebo helps on the
seen set and does nothing on the unseen set, and the placebo has no training
history at all, so whatever separates the sets is not only exposure. The
templates differ in difficulty, and the split that produced them was a hash
rather than a matching.

We report both sets separately throughout and never pool them, and we do not
read the seen-minus-unseen difference as a generalisation gap. The control arm
is what surfaced this, from inside the design, which is the argument for having
one.
