\begin{abstract}
Two open-source engines, GEPA and Microsoft's SkillOpt, automatically rewrite
the markdown ``skill'' files that agent tools load, and both accept an edit on
a single unreplicated score comparison. Their evaluations, and the wider
skill-evaluation literature, lack two cheap controls: a placebo, a document of
matched length and structure whose content says nothing, and power arithmetic
computed from the design before the run. We report the first
placebo-controlled evaluation of automated skill optimisation we are aware
of, pre-registered under a gate that refuses any published run whose
prediction cannot be shown by git ancestry to predate its data. The placebo
was in place from the first call; the power arithmetic we computed only
afterwards, and both verdicts are here.

We evolved a decision-making skill with each engine on one corpus of
computable-ground-truth decisions, against one target model,
\NUM{\studyModel}, inside one harness, then ran five arms over a holdout
minted after both winners were frozen: no skill, the hand-written seed skill,
a structure-matched placebo, and the two evolved winners. Comparisons are
McNemar exact against the placebo with Holm correction across a family of
\NUM{\familySize} per item set, on \NUM{\unseenItems} unseen and
\NUM{\seenItems} seen items analysed separately.

\textbf{No arm rejects on either item set as registered.} The best showing
is SkillOpt at $\NUM{\effectSeenSkillopt}$ on trained scenarios, raw
$p = \NUM{\pSeenSkillopt}$, which Holm takes to \NUM{\qSeenSkillopt}. Both
winners had written training-item constants into their own bodies. An A/A
control, the placebo arm scored a second time into its own checkpoint,
returned \NUM{\aaPairs} of \NUM{\aaPairs} items identical. The same records
carry a defect in our scorer, found after a first draft of this paper had read
it as a result: the target model echoed its thinking-mode switch after
\NUM{\controlTokenTotal} answers, \texttt{ANSWER: monitor /think}, the scorer
refused each as an option not on the menu, and \NUM{\controlTokenCorrectTotal}
of them named the key. The refusals fell \NUM{\controlTokenGepa} times on
GEPA's winner and \NUM{\controlTokenOff} on the empty prompt, so they moved
comparisons and not every arm together. Read with the token stripped, GEPA's winner rises
from \NUM{\accUnseenGepa} to \NUM{\rescoredAccUnseenGepa} on scenarios
neither engine saw, and on trained scenarios its comparison against the
placebo reads Holm $q = \NUM{\rescoredQSeenGepa}$ at the registered item unit
and $p = \NUM{\rescoredClusteredSeenGepa}$ uncorrected at the template unit.
We report that re-read beside the registered figures and promote neither.

\textbf{The power arithmetic decides half of that null before the data
does.} Items are drawn from templates and the template is the independent
unit, so the unseen set is three clusters, and a one-sided test over three
clusters admits no $p$ below \NUM{\floorUnseen}: all three unseen primaries
were unfalsifiable before the first call. The minimum detectable effect at
the design effect our own protocol specifies is \NUM{\mdeClusteredUnseen} on
that set, and the smaller of the two clustered figures is still
\NUM{\mdeRatio} times the largest registered gain. Both numbers were
computable from the registration alone. A companion decomposition of our own
records shows \NUM{\lowSignalTemplates} of \NUM{\studyTemplates} templates
below informedness $J = \NUM{\lowSignalThreshold}$ in every arm, so a
\NUM{\totalItems}-item run carries the discriminative signal of
\NUM{\signalItems}, and the reported metric does not even order the
templates. Each check is a few lines of arithmetic over artifacts every
study already has, and we argue all of them belong in any evaluation this
literature runs.
\end{abstract}
