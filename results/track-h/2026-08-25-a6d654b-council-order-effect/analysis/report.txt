-- what a four-draw unanimity screen does to an item of given consistency ------
  P(reject) = 1 - p^4 - (1-p)^4, for an item whose within-ordering
  consistency is p. The screen cannot tell an item that is genuinely
  unsettled from a settled item it happened to catch on a minority draw.
   consistency   P(reject)   observed in this run
        1.0000      0.0000   8/8 -- the 8 order-blind items, all admitted
        0.8750      0.4136   7/8 -- K03 under AB, REJECTED by the screen
        0.6250      0.8276   5/8 -- K09 under AB, rejected by the screen
        0.5000      0.8750   4/8 -- a true coin flip
  K03's AB behaviour is 7/8 consistent. A screen that rejects such an
  item 41% of the time is measuring its own sampling noise, not the item.
