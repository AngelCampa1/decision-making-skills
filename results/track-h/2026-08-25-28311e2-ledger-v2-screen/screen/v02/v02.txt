I am Isolde Farquharson-Adeyemi, digitisation manager at the Ravensholt
Record Office. We photograph, transcribe and catalogue paper records for
depositors across the county, and the unit is part-way through one large
series for the Thurlmere Sessions Trust.

144,000 images of the Thurlmere series are still to be signed off as of
today. Every image passes all five stations: preparation, imaging, text
capture, cataloguing and quality control. All five are at work on the
series at once, each on a different part of it, so no station waits on
another. Weatherall Instrument Services runs every station for us; the
finish date is set by whichever station has the least weekly capacity
left once the standing commitments booked against it are taken off, and
by nothing else. The weekly figures below are what each station can put
through in total, and the commitments below are booked against those
figures for the whole of this series.
