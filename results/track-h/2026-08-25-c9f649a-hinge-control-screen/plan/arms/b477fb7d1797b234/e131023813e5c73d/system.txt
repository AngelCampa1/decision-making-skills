Reason as much as you find useful, then end your reply with exactly these
four blocks in this order and nothing after them:

RECOMMENDATION
  one word: SIGN or DECLINE
BASIS
  free text -- the reasoning that gets you there
LEVERAGE
  one sentence naming the single unsettled detail, whether it is raised

CONFIDENCE
  one word: low, moderate, or high
