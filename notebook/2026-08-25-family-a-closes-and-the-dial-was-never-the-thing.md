That reading does not survive the record.

that has to be resolved through the sibling set. The rule routes on two columns,

arm**. Three blind readers named no bullet they could have dropped.

And every one of the nine readers wrote out all five subtractions. They did not

So the item got harder by every measure anyone proposed, honestly, and the

The generalisation, and it is the useful part: **a scenario compact enough to fit

## What this is not evidence about

Single-call scale is the whole of what has been tested. Nothing here says
anything about whether these procedures help at volume, over long context, under
delegation, or across a conversation — and the failures the six procedures
describe are mostly not single-call failures. That is a live hypothesis this
result does not touch, and the `docs/programme/` long-context plan already
specifies it.

**One triplet with three instances is a screen, not an estimate**, and the
registration said so before it ran. It cannot support a claim about the
population of `ledger` items. It was budgeted to decide whether to spend the next
two hundred authoring calls, and it decides that.
